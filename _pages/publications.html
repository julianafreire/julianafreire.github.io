My research develops methods and systems that enable a wide range of users to obtain trustworthy insights from data. It spans topics in data understanding, large-scale data analysis and integration, visualization, AI and machine learning, provenance management, and information discovery, addressing application areas including urban analytics, computational reproducibility, biomedical data harmonization, and AI for science.

I have co-authored over 250 papers, including 12 award winners and a Test of Time Award.
